## Overview
The "Get Transcription" endpoint allows users to retrieve transcriptions from their Toingg account. By sending a POST request to the `/get_transcription_text` endpoint, users can access specific transcriptions by specifying the API key and campaign ID. This functionality is crucial for reviewing and analyzing transcription content, ensuring that transcriptions are easily accessible for quality assurance, training, or compliance purposes. The endpoint supports efficient retrieval of transcriptions to enhance user experience and meet business requirements.
