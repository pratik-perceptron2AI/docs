## What is Toingg?
Toingg is an AI Voice Agent Platform that helps businesses automate customer conversations through smart, memory-powered voice and text agents. Unlike traditional calling systems, Toingg remembers every interaction, nurtures leads across voice and text, and schedules conversations intelligently—making every call feel human, personalized, and timely. Built as a marketplace for developers and businesses, Toingg lets you either deploy ready-to-use voice agents templates or create and customize your own—without writing complex code.

## Key Features

- **Seamless Integration:** Easily embed Toingg into your existing systems with our simple, yet powerful API.
- **Robust Security:** All interactions are secured with industry-standard authentication protocols.
- **Scalability:** Designed to grow with your business, handling both small and large-scale calling operations starting with 5 concurrent calls you can scale upto 100 for enterprises.
- **Actionable Insights:** Access comprehensive analytics and reporting tools to refine your approach.
- **Long-Term Memory**: Our agents remember previous conversations and context, picking up where they left off—across calls, texts, per campaign per phone number.
- **Goal-Based Nurturing**: Set goals for leads (e.g., book a meeting, make a payment) and Toingg's AI keeps engaging them—via voice and/or text—until the goal is achieved.
- **Smart Scheduling & Rescheduling**: Customers can request a call "after 5 minutes" or "after 5 months"—Toingg tracks, remembers, and calls back exactly when needed.
- **CRM Integration**: Toingg works out-of-the-box with Monday.com, ERP next, Zapier, Make.com, N8N and more. It updates customer records automatically after each interaction.
- **Fallback to Text**: If a voice call isn't ideal (busy tone, poor network), Toingg switches to SMS or WhatsApp automatically, reducing call drop rates by over 60%.
- **Post-Call Sentiment Analysis**: Analyze how conversations went and push the insights directly into your CRM or sales tools.

## How Toingg Works

- **Connect Your CRM / Upload Contacts**: Sync your existing contact list, or integrate via Zapier, make or API.
- **Create or Select a Campaign**: Choose a pre-built agent template (e.g., Hiring, Car Service, Real Estate) or create a new conversational flow using our No-Code Flow Builder.
- **Launch Voice + Text Campaigns**: Toingg starts engaging leads, remembers conversations, schedules callbacks, and adapts to real-time changes.
- **Analyze & Optimize**: Review campaign results, sentiment analytics, and memory logs—all synced back to your CRM.
